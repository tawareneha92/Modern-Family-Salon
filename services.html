FAQ

Have questions?

Do you take walk-ins or only appointments?+

We hold a few walk-in slots daily, but booking online guarantees your preferred time and stylist.

Can I book for my whole family in one slot?+

Yes. Add each family member and their service when you book online, and we will schedule your chairs together.

Do you offer services for children?+

Yes, children's cuts are a dedicated service with seating and pacing suited to kids.

How do I book an appointment online?+

Use the contact page to send your preferred date, time and services, and we will confirm by phone or WhatsApp.

What is included in the bridal package?+

A trial session plus hair, make-up and styling on the wedding morning at the studio.
