OUR STORY

A salon built around your family, not just your hair

Modern Family Salon started because Nanded City did not have one place that treated a father, a mother and a child as equally important guests.

HOW WE STARTED

One chair, then a family of them

We opened as a single-chair studio in Nanded City with a simple idea: a hair salon that men, women and children could all book into, on the same afternoon, without switching addresses or specialists.

Word travelled through the neighbourhood before our signage did. Today we run a small team of specialists — each trained in a specific area, from children's cuts to bridal make-up — so every member of your family sees someone who actually works in that service every day.

HOW WE WORK

Three things we do not compromise on

Considered, not rushed

Every service starts with a consultation, not a guess.

Genuinely family-first

Proper seating and patience for children, real technique for adults.

Appointments that run on time

We book realistically, so your slot starts when it says it will.
